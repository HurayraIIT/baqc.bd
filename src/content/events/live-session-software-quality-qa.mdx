## The session

BAQC hosted a live Zoom session with **Kazi Shoukat Hossain** — Manager of Projects & SQA, and an Independent Contractor for Apple — who brought **30+ years of real industry experience** to a room full of aspiring QA engineers from across Bangladesh.

### What we covered

- **Quality** — what it actually means, beyond the textbook definition
- **Quality Assurance** — how QA sits inside a real software team and why that matters
- **Software Quality Assurance** — the practical techniques that separate a good QA engineer from a great one

### About the trainer

**Kazi Shoukat Hossain**
Manager — Projects & SQA
Independent Contractor for Apple

Three decades of building and shipping software at companies where quality is non-negotiable — and generous enough to share a slice of that experience with a brand-new community.
