## A good afternoon in Mirpur

Our first in-person meetup — a **Lunch Party & Networking Meetup** at Kacchi Bhai, Mirpur 12. No slides, no agenda, just food and conversation with people building their careers in QA.

### Venue

**Kacchi Bhai**
14/11 Mithi Plaza (3rd Floor)
Mirpur 12 Bus Stand, Dhaka

### The format

- Meet and greet over kacchi biryani 🍽️
- Informal introductions — who you are, what you're working on, what you want to learn
- Networking time — because the best career moves come from knowing the right people
- A few words from the organizers about what's next for BAQC

### Why it mattered

Online events are great, but there's something about sharing a meal that an online meetup can't replicate. Watching a room full of strangers leave as friends was the best possible answer to the question "does this community have legs?"

### Cost

৳510 (including bKash charge). This covered food and venue only — BAQC itself is and will always be free to join.

### Thank you

To everyone who registered, showed up, and helped someone else feel welcome — you made this. Let's do it again soon.
