## A look back

This was BAQC's pilot workshop — the one that proved the community was needed. Over 80 aspiring testers joined from across Bangladesh for three hours of practical, hands-on learning.

### What we covered

- The anatomy of a great test case
- Equivalence partitioning and boundary value analysis (without the textbook fluff)
- How to write a bug report that developers will actually read
- A live exercise: testing a real (broken) login form

### The takeaway

Half the room had never written a formal test case before. By the end, everyone had drafted, peer-reviewed, and improved at least three. The chat was full of "I had no idea testing could be this structured" — which was exactly the point.
