## TL;DR

BAQC.bd is a fully static website. We do not run a database, we do not have user accounts, and we do not place tracking cookies. The only data we ever see is the anonymized, aggregated traffic numbers that **Cloudflare Web Analytics** provides to us. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless and does not track individuals across sites.

## What we collect

**Nothing personally identifiable.** Concretely:

- **Traffic analytics** via Cloudflare Web Analytics — page views, referrer, country, browser type. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no individual user tracking.
- **Standard server logs** that Cloudflare keeps as part of running its CDN, retained per Cloudflare's standard policy.

We do **not** collect: your name, email, IP address (beyond Cloudflare's edge logs), location beyond country level, behavioral profiles, or anything you'd reasonably call "personal data".

## Forms and contact

Our [contact page](/contact) uses a `mailto:` link, which opens your own email client. We never see your email address until you actually send us mail. If you do email us, we keep the message in our inbox like any normal email — we don't add you to a list, and we don't share your address with anyone.

## Third parties

The only third party involved in serving this site is **Cloudflare** (hosting + analytics). Their privacy policy is at [cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

We do not embed YouTube videos, Google Maps, social media SDKs, or any other third-party tracker. If we ever need to, we'll update this page first.

## Your rights

If you've emailed us and want us to delete your message, just email us back and ask. That's it.

## Changes

If we ever change anything here, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
