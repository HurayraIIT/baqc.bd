## Our mission

To be the most welcoming, beginner-friendly, and practically useful community for aspiring quality engineers in Bangladesh. We exist so that no one starting their QA journey has to start it alone.

## What we believe

- **Curiosity beats credentials.** You don't need a CS degree, certifications, or prior experience to belong here. You just need to want to learn.
- **Questions are the work.** Every "stupid question" you ask in our community is one fewer person who has to ask it alone tomorrow.
- **Practical over theoretical.** Our events teach what you'd actually do on a Monday morning at work — not what a textbook says you should do.
- **Local first, globally aware.** We're Bangladeshi by design and proud of it. We also learn from the best of the global QA community and bring it home.

## Who we serve

- Students considering quality engineering as a career
- Self-taught testers transitioning into QA
- Junior QA engineers looking for peers, mentors, and opportunities
- Experienced engineers who want to give back by mentoring or speaking

## How we work

BAQC is **100% volunteer-run** and **free to participate in**. We don't sell anything, we don't gatekeep, and we don't have membership fees. We host meetups and workshops, publish articles written by community members, and maintain this site as a public, open resource.

The site itself is open source — you can [read the code](https://github.com/baqc-bd) and contribute via pull request.

## Get involved

The best way to start is to come to an event. Check the [events page](/events) for what's next, and say hello when you arrive. If you'd like to help organize, write a blog post, or run a workshop, the [contact page](/contact) has all the ways to reach us.
