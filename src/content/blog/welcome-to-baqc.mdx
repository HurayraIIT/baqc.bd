## Why BAQC exists

Quality engineering in Bangladesh is growing fast, but we noticed something missing: a friendly, welcoming home for **aspiring** QA engineers. Most communities cater to seniors. Most beginners learn alone.

**BAQC** changes that. We're building the community we wished existed when we were starting out — where the only prerequisite is curiosity, and the questions everyone is too afraid to ask are the ones we celebrate.

## What you'll find here

- **Monthly meetups** — both online and in Dhaka — covering testing fundamentals, automation, performance, and career growth.
- **A blog** written by the community, for the community. Real stories from real testers.
- **A timeline of events** so you never miss what's next.
- **A team** of volunteer organizers who genuinely care about helping people break into the field.

## How to join

We're on every major platform. Drop into any of our channels — listed on the [contact page](/contact) — say hello, and tell us what you're working on. That's it. There are no application forms, no membership tiers, no gatekeeping.

If you'd like to **speak at a meetup**, **write a post**, or **propose a workshop**, reach out the same way. We move fast for people who want to contribute.

## What's next

Our first community event is coming soon — keep an eye on the [events page](/events). And if you read this and felt like it was written for you, it probably was. Welcome home.
